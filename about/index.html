A little about Claw.

Hi, I’m Charles, but most people call me Claw. I like understanding how things work — and figuring out how they could work better for people.

Engineering, with people in mind.

I’m Charles Law — Claw for short — a software engineer in Los Angeles with a background in electrical and systems engineering. Over the past two decades, I’ve worked on radar algorithms, distributed data platforms, and AI systems used in healthcare.

I work between engineering and research: understanding a hard problem, turning an idea into working software, and staying close to the people who depend on it. The most satisfying part is turning a technical possibility into a tool that makes someone’s day easier.

At Eyenuk, my work has spanned production software, clinical review tools, data quality, and the systems that bring AI into real clinical workflows. That means working with researchers, clinicians, customers, and engineers — and making their feedback part of the product.

Outside of work, I’m a husband and father, a reader, and someone who tends to find another engineering project to tinker with. Some of those experiments are collected on the projects page.

A few threads through my work

Make the complicated useful.

From noisy radar data to medical images, I’ve spent much of my career helping turn complex information into something people can act on.

Build the connection.

I enjoy the space between research, product, and engineering — especially when working directly with users helps reveal what’s actually needed.

Keep a little curiosity.

A shape-recognition tool, a programming language, a puzzle game: side projects are a good way to follow an interesting question and learn something new.
